Add a Tools menu to the PolicyEngine header, right after Research, built by the same rule as policyengine.org's: the PolicyEngine app first, then every calculator that works for any household under current law, sorted by label. The US menu lists CliffWatch, Coverage Compass, GiveCalc, the marriage calculator, the QBI deduction calculator, the SPM threshold calculator and the TANF calculator; the UK menu lists the marriage calculator, the student loan calculator and UK CliffWatch; other countries get no menu. Every row is a full page load, because the PolicyEngine app is on its own domain and each tool runs as its own app on policyengine.org. New exports: `policyEngineTools`, `getPolicyEngineTools`, `getPolicyEngineAppUrl`, `POLICYENGINE_APP_LABEL`, `DEFAULT_POLICYENGINE_APP_URL` and the `PolicyEngineTool` and `NavItemChildConfig` types. `getPolicyEngineNavItems` takes an optional app URL as its third argument, and `PolicyEngineHeader` an `appUrl` prop. `NavItemConfig` items and their dropdown rows take a new `external` flag that renders a plain link, bypassing `linkComponent` and `onNavigate`; rows inherit it from their item unless they set their own.
